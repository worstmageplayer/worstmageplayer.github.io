= Week 1
== Main Thermal Zone in a cement kiln
===  Dehydration Zone, #C(500) -- #C(750)
- Evaporation and removal of water.
=== Calcination Zone, #C(500) -- #C(950)
- Decomposing of limestone into carbon dioxide gas and free lime (CaO).
- At the end, mix consists of oxides of four main elements.
    - CaO
    - SiO#sub[2]
    - Al#sub[2]O#sub[3]
    - Fe#sub[2]O#sub[3]
=== Solid State Reaction Zone, #C(950) -- #C(1350)
- CaO reacts with silica to form C#sub[2]S.
- Intermediate calcium aluminates and calcium ferrite compounds form.
    - These compounds play an important role as fluxing agents.
=== Clinkering Zone, #C(1350) -- #C(1500)
- Formation of C#sub[3]S occurs.
- Melting of calcium aluminate and ferrite phases creates a liquid phase, causing raw mix to form clinker nodules.
- C#sub[3]S crystals grow while C#sub[2]S crystals decrease.
- Clinkering process is complete when:
    - Silica is present as C#sub[3] and C#sub[2].
    - Free lime, CaO, is reduced to \<1%.

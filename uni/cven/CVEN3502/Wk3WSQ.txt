== Workshop Question 1
A wastewater treatment plant produces thickened primary sludge at a rate of 5000 kg/day. This thickened sludge contains 5% solids by mass, of which 80% is volatile solids.

A Biochemical Methane Potential (BMP) test for this thickened sludge shows a total methane yield of 250 NmL/gVS.

The thickened sludge is to be processed by anaerobic digestion and methane gas is to be recovered.

#enum(numbering: "a)", start: 1)[
If the digesters operate with 85% efficiency for volatile solids degradation, determine the potential volumetric methane production rate.]

The size of the anaerobic digesters is such that small additional volume (about 10%) is available, in addition to the volume occupied by 5000 kg/day thickened sludge. The plant operators believe they can increase overall methane production by 'co-digestion' with 500 kg/day of available food waste.

The food waste has a total solids content of 30%, of which 91% is volatile solids. The BMP is determined to be 519 NmL/gVS, and the treatment efficiency is 95%.

#enum(numbering: "a)", start: 2)[
Calculate the total potential methane production (volume per day) from 5000 kg/day thickened sludge mixed with 500 kg/day food waste.]

Methane has a molecular mass of 16 g/mol and a molar volume (at standard temperature and pressure) of 22.4 L/mol.

#enum(numbering: "a)", start: 3)[
Calculate the daily mass of methane produced.]
=== Solution:
#set enum(numbering: "a)")
+ $ "Q"_("CH"_4) &= "M" dot "TS" dot "VS" dot "BMP" dot eta\
&= 5000 dot 10^3 frac("g", "day", style:"horizontal") dot 0.05 dot 0.8 dot 250 frac("NmL", "gVS", style:"horizontal") dot 0.85 \
&= 42,500 frac("L", "day", style:"horizontal") $
+ $ "Q"_"food waste" &= "M" dot "TS" dot "VS" dot "BMP" dot eta\
&= 500 dot 10^3 frac("g", "day", style:"horizontal") dot 0.3 dot 0.91 dot 519 frac("NmL", "gVS", style:"horizontal") dot 0.95 \
&= 67,301 frac("L", "day", style:"horizontal")\
"Q"_"total" &= "Q"_("CH"_4) + "Q"_"food waste"\
&= 42,500 + 67,301\
&= 109.801 frac("L", "day", style:"horizontal") $
+ Convert volume to moles:
    $ "n" &= "V"/"V"_"m"\
    &= (109,801 frac("L", "day", style:"horizontal")) / (22.4 frac("L", "mol", style:"horizontal"))\
    &= 4902 frac("mol", "day", style:"horizontal") $
    Convert moles to mass:
    $ "m" &= "n" dot "MV" \
    &= 4902 frac("mol", "day", style:"horizontal") dot 16 frac("g", "mol", style:"horizontal") dot 10^(-3) frac("g", "kg", style:"horizontal")\
    &= 78 frac("kg", "day", style:"horizontal")$

- Specific gravity: 1.022
- Moisture fraction: 0.850
- Volatile fraction of total solids: 0.761

- carbon (C) = 12.0 g/mol
- hydrogen (H) = 1.00 g/mol

All gasses have a molar volume (at standard temperature and pressure) of 22.4 L mol#super[-1].

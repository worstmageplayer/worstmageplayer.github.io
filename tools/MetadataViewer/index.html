File support & privacy

AI helped build this tool. Your files are processed locally in your browser, without being sent to an AI service.

Detailed image tags: JPEG, TIFF, PNG, HEIC, AVIF, WebP, GIF and JPEG XL. Audio and video are inspected with MediaInfo. PDF document properties, ZIP directories, Office/OpenDocument/EPUB properties and TTF/OTF/WOFF font names are supported. Other formats may return basic information only. WOFF2 and camera RAW coverage are limited.

Files are read in memory, never uploaded or modified. Closing this page clears the session. Browser selection does not provide the original full path or filesystem creation date. Embedded metadata can be missing or inaccurate. Dependencies are served with this page; no external services are used.
